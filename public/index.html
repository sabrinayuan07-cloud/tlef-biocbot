Most study tools hand you the answer. BiocBot asks you to explain it back. Explaining a topic out loud is how you find out whether you are ready for the exam, and it is the hardest thing to practise on your own.

01 · Dual modes

Being taught, and doing the teaching

In tutor mode BiocBot walks you through the unit. In protégé mode it plays the student and you do the explaining. Teaching a topic back is one of the most reliable ways to find out what you have not learned yet, and you can do it here at two in the morning without booking anyone else's time.

Tutor mode

BiocBot walks you through it

Hints before answers, reasoning you can follow line by line, and questions pitched at where you actually are in the unit rather than where the syllabus says you should be.

Protégé mode

You do the explaining

BiocBot plays the student who hasn't got it yet. It asks the simple questions, pushes back when an answer is vague, and stops exactly where your explanation runs out.

03 · Overview

Overview

Instructors put their own content in, and students join the classes they are taking. Every BiocBot answer comes from that real course material, so you are not left wondering where it came from the way you are with a general AI tool. Do still check it against your notes.

01

The instructor loads the content

Slides, notes and readings go in directly. BiocBot draws on those, so its answers stay inside what the course has actually covered.

02

You join your classes

Pick the courses you are taking and enter the join code your instructor shares with the class.

03

A module is released

The second your instructor posts the module slides for students to access, you can start practicing and reviewing them.

04

Work through the unit

Each unit comes with its own set of questions. Answer them with BiocBot tutoring you, or take the other side and explain them yourself.

The toggle

The toggle sits right in the chat box, next to where you type. Flip it mid-session and BiocBot changes sides with you, so you are never stuck in one mode for a whole study session.

Keeping track

BiocBot was built so that students and instructors can both see how well a particular learning objective or content theme has been understood, rather than guessing at it from a midterm.

04 · Research

The study buddy program

A study buddy pilot at UBC paired around 1,300 students across several classes, so everyone took a turn tutoring and a turn being tutored. Students who took part finished with grade averages 1.5% above those who did not, and in some classes the gap was as wide as 5.5%.

Even so, about half the students in every class never got to join. Their work shifts clashed with the meeting times, or the commute made it impossible, or they had family to look after. BiocBot exists so that studying this way no longer depends on two people being free in the same room at the same time.

Read the study buddy paper

Jones et al., 2025. "Study buddy learning is associated with academic success in undergraduate science courses." bioRxiv 2025.05.20.655056.
